# Maharashtra Government Digital Services Portal

## Goal
Create a polished single-page concept that combines the formal Maharashtra Government / Maha BOCW visual identity with horizontally browsable portal cards.

## Visual direction
- Recreate the reference site's official hierarchy: charcoal utility strip, white government identity header, deep maroon navigation, and restrained light content areas.
- Use the real Maharashtra Government emblem/logo sourced from the reference site; do not invent marks.
- Keep typography formal and highly readable, with compact navigation and limited motion.
- Use modest corners, thin borders, subtle shadows, and section accents rather than SaaS-style panels or dramatic effects.

## Page structure
1. Utility bar with Government of Maharashtra bilingual identity and language control.
2. Main identity header with emblem, bilingual Maharashtra Government branding, portal title, and search control.
3. Maroon navigation bar with simple portal links.
4. Introductory title and supplied subtitle.
5. Four horizontal, OTT-inspired card rows: Education & Learning, Worker Services, Skill Development, and Government Services.
6. Official-looking footer with the requested links and copyright.

## Card behavior
- Each card includes a themed image/logo area, portal title, short description, and “Visit Portal →”.
- Unacademy opens `https://unacademy.com/` in a new tab.
- Other destinations use clearly grouped placeholder URLs so they are easy to replace.
- Rows remain swipeable on mobile and show a spacious multi-card layout on desktop.

## Responsive and accessibility
- Collapse the full navigation into a compact mobile menu.
- Preserve readable text, stable card sizes, keyboard focus states, semantic landmarks, and reduced-motion support.
- Add page-specific search/social metadata and meaningful image alt text.

## Validation
- Check the final desktop and mobile views for layout, overflow, card interaction, official branding, and the Unacademy destination.
